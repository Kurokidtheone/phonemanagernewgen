Your signing key

The signing key is what makes your license keys genuine. Create it once, then load the same file every time.

Creating a new one gives you two files. license-private.pem stays secret: back it up offline and never share it. license-public.pem goes inside the app. Keys signed with a different signing key will not activate your app.
